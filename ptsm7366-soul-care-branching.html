A player stays behind after practice. Choose what you say at each turn. The skills you're practising are attending, responding, personalizing, and initiating — in that order, and only as far as the athlete lets you go.

Recap — the Art of Helping:

  • Attend before you respond — presence first, then reflection.
  • Respond to content, then feeling, before you personalize.
  • Initiate only a step the athlete names as hers.
