PTSM 7366 Soul Care in Sports

Interactive activity previews for faculty review · 15 activities, one per week

How to review: open each activity and try it as a student would. Then choose Use it, Don't use it, or Request a change under it, here or at the bottom of the activity, and add a note if you like. When you're done, select Copy my choices and paste them into an email to keith_pace@baylor.edu. Your choices are saved in this browser as you go. These previews don't record anything about how you do in the activities.
Week 1

Our Class Covenant

Guided Documentation

Students complete a guided form with 6 prompts in 3 sections, then copy or download their responses.

Outcome: Commit to the class covenant and norms.

Open activity
Week 2

Soul Care: Key Terms

Flashcards

Students practice 9 flashcards, recalling each before turning it; missed cards come back until recalled.

Outcome: Understand terms, definitions, concepts and practices related to a practical theology of soul care.

Open activity
Week 3

Where Does My Worth Come From?

Drag and Drop

Students sort 10 items into 2 categories (Performance-based identity, Identity grounded in God's favor), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Analyze Christian approaches for how sportspeople can cultivate a healthy and holistic identity.

Open activity
Week 4

Who Cares for the Athlete?

Interactive Map

Students open 8 role cards, each describing a caregiver's part in athlete well-being with a question to consider.

Outcome: Consider the variety of roles within sports that provide support and care for athletes as people.

Open activity
Week 5

The Chaplain's Role

Drag and Drop

Students sort 11 items into 2 categories (Fits the sports chaplain's role, Belongs to another role (partner or refer)), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Understand what sports chaplaincy is and how it is practiced.

Open activity
Week 6

A Conversation After Practice

Branching Scenario

A soul-care conversation with an athlete using the Art of Helping skills; branches on attending, responding and challenging choices.

Outcome: Practice listening and self-reflection.

Open activity
Week 7

Formation Practices in Sport

Dialog Cards

Students turn 6 cards and work through each card's reflection prompts, with an optional note.

Outcome: Evaluate models of discipleship within sports.

Open activity
Week 8

Listening to Someone in Pain

Mark the Words

Students read a short conversation and mark the 5 responses that minimize or rush past the athlete's pain, with feedback on each.

Outcome: Identify best practices for listening well when others are experiencing suffering and loss.

Open activity
Week 9

Walk With, Refer, or Act Now

Drag and Drop

Students sort 9 items into 3 categories (Listen and walk alongside, Refer to a licensed professional, Act now: get emergency help), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Consider and apply best practices for providing support and care for those with mental health challenges.

Open activity
Week 10

Faith and Performance: Debate Planner

Guided Documentation

Students complete a guided form with 9 prompts in 4 sections, then copy or download their responses.

Outcome: Engage in research and develop an argument based on evidence.

Open activity
Week 11

Open, Closed or Leading?

Dropdown

Students complete 9 blanks by choosing from dropdown menus, then check and fix any that are wrong.

Outcome: Develop questions for the panelists that draw on course content and themes.

Open activity
Week 12

Car-Ride and Sideline Habits

Drag and Drop

Students sort 10 items into 2 categories (Supports the young athlete, Adds pressure or takes over), with feedback and a retry on anything misplaced.

Outcome: Connect what the parents panel shares with the themes and topics of the course.

Open activity
Week 13

Soul Care Vocabulary Crossword

Crossword

Students solve a 12-word crossword.

Outcome: Connect what the chaplain panel shares with the themes and topics of the course.

Open activity
Week 14

A Coach and a Benched Player

Branching Scenario

Students make choices through a branching scenario (8 decision points, 3 possible endings), with feedback after every choice.

Outcome: Assess and evaluate key ideas, practices and perspectives shared by coaches and athletic directors.

Open activity
Week 15

Listening Project Presentation Planner

Guided Documentation

Students complete a guided form with 10 prompts in 4 sections, then copy or download their responses.

Outcome: Highlight the most important takeaways from the Listening Project interviews.

Open activity
